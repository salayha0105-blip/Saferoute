This is the questionnaire we **would** give to women who live, study or work in Gulberg III.
For this course prototype it was **not** given to real people. The answers in
`simulated_survey_responses.csv` are **SIMULATED** (see `SIMULATED_SURVEY.md`).

Anonymous: no name, phone number or exact address is asked.
